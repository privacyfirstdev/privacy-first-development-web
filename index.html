Secure by design

The extension collects nothing about your chats, so there is nothing to leak, sell, or lose.

01

Your chats stay in your browser

No analytics, no tracking, no ads. Your username, chat names and messages never leave your browser. The only network requests are for the license, described in the Privacy Policy.

02

Only the tab you pick

It gets local access to the active tab when you open the extension. It does not ask for access to any website except its own license server.

03

Test before delete

Test mode is on by default. It counts matching messages and deletes nothing until you turn it off.

No analytics. No tracking. No message data leaves your browser.

How a run works

Four steps, all inside one tab.

  1. Open reddit.com/chat in a tab.
  2. Click the extension icon and type your Reddit username, without u/.
  3. Press Check page, then run a test mode and read the counts.
  4. When you are ready, turn off test mode and press Start.

The free plan covers 3 chats per day. To remove that limit, buy a lifetime license from inside the extension.
