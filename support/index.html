Support: Delete My Chat Messages for Reddit

For help, questions, bug reports, feature requests, license problems, or refunds, email us:

support@privacyfirstdevelopment.com

We read every message and reply by email. You do not need an account anywhere to get support.

Not working at all?

Reddit changes its chat page without notice, and the extension reads that page, so an update on Reddit's side can stop it from working. If it stops finding chats or messages, email us. Fixes ship as extension updates, so check that you are on the latest version.

Reporting a bug

Please include:

  • Extension version. Shown on chrome://extensions.
  • Chrome version. Shown on chrome://version.
  • Whether test mode was on. The button reads "Count matches" in test mode and "Start deleting" when test mode is off.
  • What happened. What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Steps to reproduce. For example: open reddit.com/chat, click the extension icon, then what you did next.
  • Diagnostics. The output of the "Copy diagnostics" button in the popup. It holds no usernames, chat names, or message text.

Suggesting a feature

Tell us what problem you are trying to solve and what you would like to see.

Keep it private

Please do not send private chat content, screenshots of conversations, your Reddit password, or payment card details. Describe what happened in your own words instead. We never need any of them.

Licenses and refunds

For a license problem, include the email address you used at checkout. To move a license to a new device, free up a slot (a license works on up to 3 browsers or devices), or have your license record deleted, email us. Refund terms are in the Terms of Use.
