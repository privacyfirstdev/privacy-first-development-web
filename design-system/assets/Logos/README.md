Logo files. `mark.svg` and `lockup.svg` are the current artwork, drawn as clean geometry with the wordmark outlined from Figtree: the blue is `logo-blue` and the orange is `logo-orange`. `mark-original-traced.svg` is the multi-colour trace of the original with its shield outline in `logo-green`, and `original-lockup.png` is the supplied lockup at 1200 pixels wide. None of them are single-ink, so they cannot take a text colour.
