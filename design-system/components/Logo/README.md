The primary Privacy First Development mark and lockup: blue and orange brackets around a person. Use `mark.svg` on its own and `lockup.svg` when the name is needed. The shield version is only for places that specifically require it.

Place it on `surface-100`, leaving `space-6` of clear space on every side. On the dark theme it can sit directly on `surface-100`; on other grounds use a white panel with `radius-lg`. Provide the mark as an SVG from `assets/Logos`; do not redraw, stretch, outline or add effects. Do not use the orange from the logo for small text.
